# NLP Programs 01–10 — Problem Statements

## Program 01 — Basic NLP Text Preprocessing Pipeline

### Problem Statement

Given a piece of raw text containing uppercase and lowercase words, punctuation, and common English stopwords, build an NLP preprocessing pipeline that converts the raw text into a clean sequence of normalized words using basic text preprocessing techniques.

---

## Program 02 — Regex-Based Text Cleaning

### Problem Statement

Given a piece of raw text containing URLs, email addresses, numbers, special characters, hashtags, mentions, and extra spaces, clean the text using Python Regular Expressions (`re`).

---

## Program 03 — Stemming vs Lemmatization

### Problem Statement

Given a collection of words and sentences containing different forms of the same or related words, apply stemming and lemmatization to normalize the text and compare how both techniques transform the words.

---

## Program 04 — Word Frequency Analyzer

### Problem Statement

Given a paragraph of text, analyze the frequency of each word and determine which words occur most frequently in the text after basic preprocessing.

---

## Program 05 — Bag of Words from Scratch

### Problem Statement

Given a collection of text documents, create a Bag of Words representation from scratch by building a vocabulary of unique words and representing each document as a numerical vector based on the frequency of words appearing in that document.

---

## Program 06 — N-Gram Text Generator

### Problem Statement

Given a sentence or collection of sentences, generate unigram, bigram, and trigram representations of the text to capture individual words and consecutive sequences of words.

---

## Program 07 — TF-IDF from Scratch

### Problem Statement

Given a collection of text documents, calculate the TF-IDF representation of the words in each document from scratch by determining how frequently a word appears within a document and how important or rare that word is across the entire document collection.

---

## Program 08 — Cosine Similarity for Text

### Problem Statement

Given two or more text documents, convert the documents into numerical vector representations and calculate their cosine similarity to determine how similar the documents are to one another.

---

## Program 09 — Simple Text Classification

### Problem Statement

Given a labeled dataset of text documents belonging to different categories, build a basic text classification system that learns from the labeled examples and predicts the category of a new, previously unseen text.

---

## Program 10 — Sentiment Analysis

### Problem Statement

Given a collection of text reviews or sentences labeled with their sentiment, build a sentiment analysis system that determines whether a new piece of text expresses a positive, negative, or neutral sentiment.
